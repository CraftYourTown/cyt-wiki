# Minions

{% hint style="info" %}
This feature is exclusive to our **Skyblock** server
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Minions can be utilised to perform repetitive actions automatically.

### Types of Minion

* Miner Minion - automatically mines what's in front of them
* Seller Minion - automatically sells to the shop from a linked container
* Collector Minion - automatically picks up ground items and stores in a linked container
* Farmer Minion - automatically farms around them
* Feeder Minion - automatically feeds nearby minions
* Lumberjack Minion - automatically cuts tree that's in front of them
* Slayer Minion - automatically kills mobs within range

### Upgrades

Minions all have their own unique upgrade paths, including range, smelting, fortune, and plenty more. You can see what your next upgrade is via your minion manager menu.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### How do I get minions?

Minions can be obtained through Daily Rewards, as well as Uncommon, Rare, and Mango Crates.

### Island Limits

Your island will limit how many minions you can have, you can increase this via the `/upgrades` menu.
